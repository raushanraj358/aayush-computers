Privacy Policy

Aayush Computers (the "App", "we", "us") is a sales and service app and website run by Aayush Computers, owner Nitish Kamlapuri. Customers use it to send sales enquiries, book repairs for computers, printers and CCTV, place orders, and track the status of each request.

Effective date: 19 November 2026. Home page: https://raushanraj358.github.io/aayush-computers/

1. Information we collect

From Google Sign-In

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive only your name, email address and profile picture (Google scopes: email, profile, openid). We do not access your Gmail messages, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.

From you directly

  • Your name, and your mobile number or email address, if you create an account with them. We send a one-time code by SMS or email to confirm the account and to reset a forgotten password.
  • Your password. It is stored by Supabase only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody, including the shop, can read it.
  • Your mobile number, if you give it for repair updates.
  • Request details you type in: the device or product, its problem or your requirement, and your delivery or pickup details.
  • Your order and repair history and the status of each request.

Collected automatically

  • A device token, used only to send you app notifications.
  • Basic technical logs needed to keep the service secure and working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To receive and process your enquiries, repairs and orders, and show you their status.
  • To send you SMS and app notifications each time your request changes status.
  • To let the shop owner see and manage incoming requests and contact you about them.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google name, email and profile picture only to create and identify your account in the App. We do not transfer this data to others except as needed to provide the service (see section 4), we do not use it for advertising, and no person reads it except the shop owner for the purpose of serving your requests, or where required by law.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers that run the App, and only so they can provide their service:

  • Supabase: stores accounts, requests and order history, and handles sign-in.
  • Google: provides Google Sign-In and app notifications.
  • An SMS provider: delivers text messages to your mobile number.

Your requests are visible only to you and to the shop owner. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and request history while your account is active and as needed for repair, warranty and order records. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your personal information, except where the law requires us to keep certain records.

6. Security

Data is stored with Supabase using access controls so that customers can see only their own requests. Data travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can view your requests and their history in the App.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your account and data at any time by calling 90064 34998 or visiting the shop.
  • You can stop SMS and app notifications by telling us, or by turning off notifications in your phone settings.
  • You can withdraw Google access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version at this address.
