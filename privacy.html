Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Ayush Computer ("we", "us") runs an app and website for sales enquiries, repair requests, orders and status tracking. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and mobile number, from Google sign-in or the mobile number you give us.
  • Request details: the device or product, the problem or requirement you describe, and your order and repair history.
  • Notification details: a device token used to send app alerts, and your mobile number for SMS updates.

How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To handle your enquiries, repairs and orders, and show you their status.
  • To send you SMS and app notifications about your requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Only you and the shop owner can see your requests. We use trusted service providers to run the app: Supabase (database and login), Google (sign-in) and an SMS provider (text messages). They process data only to provide those services.

Your choices
